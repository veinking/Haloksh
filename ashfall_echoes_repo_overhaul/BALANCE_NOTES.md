# Balance Notes (Patch 10)

## Baseline
- Player base HP: 72
- Starting Echoes: 20
- Starting deck target: 10 cards from loadout/weapon starters

## Card assumptions
- Basic Strike: 6 (9 upgraded)
- Basic Guard: 5 block (8 upgraded)
- Commons are efficient, uncommons add synergies, rares define direction.

## Enemy assumptions
- Act 1 normal attacks mostly 5-10.
- Act 1 elite attacks mostly 9-18.
- Act 1 boss attacks mostly 10-24 with phase pressure.

## Reward assumptions
- Normal: 15-25 Echoes + card reward.
- Elite: 35-55 Echoes + card reward + relic.
- Boss: large Echoes + boss relic.

## Open tuning questions
- Burn decay pressure against defensive loadouts may still spike too hard.
- Some generated archetype cards are intentionally simple and may need bespoke effects later.
- Boss relic downside tuning may need additional hook support.

## Playtest checklist
- Clear first 3 combats with baseline loadout.
- Confirm shop appears and is affordable by mid-act.
- Confirm at least one rest appears before boss.
- Verify both Act 1 bosses can appear.
- Verify elite rewards include relics.
- Verify events meaningfully alter run trajectory.
- Confirm old save loads without crash.
